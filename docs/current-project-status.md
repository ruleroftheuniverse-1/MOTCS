# Current project status

## Scientific result

The rate-equation and numerical machinery has been reproduced, but the published force structure has not. The remaining discrepancy is localized to the molecular-model objects supplied to the equations.

Reproduced: equilibrium rate equations, pylcp behavior for identical inputs, optical and magnetic conventions, Gaussian geometry, interpolation, event-aware integration, and complex/basis-phase fidelity. Not reproduced: the force topology, magnitude, spatial width, and component `(4)` hierarchy in Rodriguez Figures 2 and 3. Therefore exact MgF replication, capture, physical policy comparisons, and optimization remain locked.

This is the strongest supported conclusion—not generic “ongoing validation.” The independent paper-equation evaluator and pylcp agree, while both disagree with the paper when supplied the accepted provisional molecular objects.
